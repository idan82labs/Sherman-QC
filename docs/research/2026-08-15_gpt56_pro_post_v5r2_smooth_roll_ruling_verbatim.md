# RULING: EXECUTE_ONE

**Authorize exactly one prospective intervention:**

`V63_LOCALIZED_TURN_CORRIDOR_OWNERSHIP_V1`
abbreviation: `LTCO_V1`

V63 is a **deletion-only, source-cloud curvature-localization certificate applied exclusively to final V52-origin recoveries**. It must not alter V16 lines, create candidates, reorder candidates, release NMS-suppressed candidates, or invoke NMS again.

The consumed O001/O003/O005/O007 scans must not be executed through V63, including as an unscored “sanity check.” They remain diagnosis only.

## 1. Physical invariant

For a candidate bend direction (a), take a surface profile across the sheet, transverse to (a), and parameterize it by surface arclength (u). Let (\theta(u)) be the surface-normal angle after projecting normals into the plane perpendicular to (a).

Then

[
q(u)=\left|\frac{d\theta}{du}\right|
]

is the magnitude of transverse normal curvature.

A **localized bend corridor** must produce:

1. a spatially compact, predominantly monotone change in (\theta(u));
2. a bounded lobe in (q(u));
3. low-curvature shoulders on both sides;
4. a lobe center coincident with the proposed line;
5. persistence of the same lobe at two smoothing scales and along the line.

A **broad smooth roll** instead normally produces a roughly linear or slowly changing (\theta(u)), hence diffuse or nearly constant (q(u)), without a compact, uniquely owned, shoulder-bounded lobe.

This is the correct invariant to test. “Cylindrical support exists at two scales” is not sufficient, because both a localized bend and a rolled wall can satisfy that statement.

The exact numerical certificate below is a Sherman-specific falsifiable hypothesis. It is not claimed as a theorem that separates every large-radius bend from every rolled surface.

## 3. Inputs and bounded profile construction

### 3.1 Permitted inputs

For each V52-origin final line:

* line point (c);
* unit line direction (a);
* frozen axial support interval ([s_{\min},s_{\max}]);
* existing `two_sided_angle`, denoted (\Theta_c), in radians;
* its member `cylindrical_wide` source records and their frozen transverse support radius (r_{\text{wide}});
* transient source-support point indices already generated by those sources;
* bounded source coordinates (P);
* the exact deterministic normals already used upstream by the cylindrical detectors.

No normals may be recomputed with a new estimator in this tranche.

If (\Theta_c), (r_{\text{wide}}), source support, or upstream normals are absent or nonfinite, reject the V52 line.

### 3.2 Scan spacing

Compute one deterministic spacing value per scan:

1. (M=\min(N,4096)).
2. Select source indices

[
i_m=\left\lfloor \frac{(m+0.5)N}{M}\right\rfloor,\qquad m=0,\ldots,M-1.
]

3. For each selected point, find its sixth nearest neighbor in the full bounded cloud.
4. Let (d) be the median of those sixth-neighbor distances.

All searches and reductions use ascending source index for ties.

Let (B) be the maximum scan bounding-box span.

### 3.3 Axial stations

Use nine stations:

[
x_j=c+
\left[
s_{\min}+
(0.10+0.10j)(s_{\max}-s_{\min})
\right]a,
\qquad j=0,\ldots,8.
]

No station may be moved in response to geometry or dropout.

### 3.4 Profile extent

Let

[
\bar r_{\text{wide}}
====================

\operatorname{median}
{r_{\text{wide},m}:m\text{ is a member source}}.
]

The maximum requested half-profile length is

[
R_0=\min(2\bar r_{\text{wide}},,64d,,0.10B).
]

If (R_0<12d), reject the candidate.

### 3.5 Routed-profile primitive

Reuse only the repository’s existing deterministic **geometry-only routed-profile sampler**. Do not invoke its old `rolled_or_mixed_transition`, long-strip, doublet, or any other classification predicates.

At each station request:

```text
axial slab half-width: 3d
maximum arclength per side: R0
uniform output spacing: 2d
maximum raw slice points: 4096
maximum resampled points: 65 total
anchor: this candidate’s transient source-support points
required output: one connected, single-skin, unbranched ordered profile
```

When more than 4096 raw points enter a slice, retain the first 4096 under the frozen ordering:

```text
(distance_to_station, source_point_index)
```

The sampler must return ordered pairs

[
(u_k,n_k),
]

where (u=0) is the closest routed surface point to the candidate line and (u) is signed surface arclength.

Crop to a symmetric interval

[
[-R_j,R_j],\qquad
R_j=\min(R_{\text{observed,left}},R_{\text{observed,right}},R_0).
]

A station is invalid when:

* (R_j<12d);
* either side is absent;
* the route branches;
* two skins cannot be separated;
* a topology gap prevents one connected profile;
* fewer than 13 uniformly spaced samples remain;
* more than 10% of samples have

[
\left|n_k-(n_k\cdot a)a\right|<0.866.
]

Invalid stations count as failed stations. There is no fallback to V52 scalar features.

At most 32 V52-origin final lines may be audited per scan. Exceeding this bound is a `CAPACITY_FAIL` for the prospective run, not permission to process a selected subset.

## 4. Deterministic turn-profile certificate

### 4.1 Normal-angle profile

For each valid profile, project the normals perpendicular to the candidate direction:

[
m_k=
\frac{n_k-(n_k\cdot a)a}
{\left|n_k-(n_k\cdot a)a\right|}.
]

Resolve sign sequentially:

[
m_k\leftarrow
\begin{cases}
-m_k,&m_{k-1}\cdot m_k<0,\
m_k,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
]

Set (\theta_0=0), and unwrap by

[
\delta_k=
\operatorname{atan2}
\left(
a\cdot(m_k\times m_{k+1}),
m_k\cdot m_{k+1}
\right),
]

[
\theta_{k+1}=\theta_k+\delta_k.
]

Reversing the route or the sign of (a) does not affect the absolute certificate below.

### 4.2 Two frozen scales

Use the symmetric binomial kernel

[
h=\frac{1}{16}[1,4,6,4,1]
]

with reflected boundaries.

Define:

[
\theta^{f}=h*\theta,
\qquad
\theta^{c}=h*\theta^{f}.
]

These are the fine and coarse profiles.

For (r\in{f,c}), define edge turn mass

[
q^r_k=
\left|
\theta^r_{k+1}-\theta^r_k
\right|.
]

### 4.3 Lobe extraction at one scale

For either (q^r):

1. Let (k_0) be the edge midpoint closest to (u=0).
2. Find the maximum (q^r_p) among (k_0-3,\ldots,k_0+3).
3. Resolve ties by smallest absolute edge-midpoint coordinate and then lower index.
4. Set (H_r=q^r_p). If (H_r\le0), fail.
5. Walk left from (p) until encountering the nearest run of three consecutive edges for which

[
q^r_k\le0.25H_r
]

for all three edges.
6. Find the equivalent nearest three-edge run on the right.
7. If either run is absent, fail.
8. Define (I_r) as the interval between the inner boundaries of the two shoulder runs.

For (I_r), calculate:

[
Q_r=\sum_{k\in I_r}q^r_k,
]

[
T_r=
\left|
\sum_{k\in I_r}
(\theta^r_{k+1}-\theta^r_k)
\right|,
]

[
M_r=\frac{T_r}{Q_r},
]

[
\mu_r=
\frac{\sum_{k\in I_r}
u_{k+\frac12}q^r_k}
{Q_r},
]

and let (w_r) be the arclength width of (I_r).

The lobe passes at that scale only when all are true:

[
M_r\ge0.85,
]

[
T_r\ge0.50\Theta_c,
]

[
\frac{w_r}{2R_j}\le0.30,
]

[
|\mu_r|
\le
\max(2d,0.20w_r).
]

The monotonicity ratio (M_r) rejects profiles whose apparent curvature energy is caused mainly by alternating noise or overlapping surfaces.

### 4.4 Fine/coarse persistence

A station passes only if both lobes pass and:

[
\operatorname{IoU}(I_f,I_c)\ge0.60,
]

[
|\mu_f-\mu_c|\le2d,
]

[
\frac{2}{3}\le\frac{w_c}{w_f}\le\frac{3}{2},
]

and the signed turns over (I_f) and (I_c) have the same sign.

### 4.5 Candidate decision

A V52 line passes LTCO only when:

1. at least seven of nine stations pass;
2. over the passing stations,

[
\operatorname{median}(T_c)\ge0.70\Theta_c;
]

3. the coarse-scale centers satisfy

[
\operatorname{median}(|\mu_c|)\le d;
]

4. the coarse normalized widths satisfy

[
\operatorname{median}
\left(\frac{w_c}{2R_j}\right)
\le0.25;
]

5. corridor width is axially stable:

[
\frac{1.4826,
\operatorname{median}
\left(
|w_c-\operatorname{median}(w_c)|
\right)}
{\operatorname{median}(w_c)}
\le0.35.
]

Every constant above is frozen before V5R3 geometry generation. None may be changed after seeing activation counts, visual precommit results, evaluator truth, or score.

## 5. Fail-closed behavior and counterexamples

### Fail closed

A V52-origin line is rejected for any of the following:

* missing source support or wide-source scale;
* invalid or nonfinite source fields;
* ambiguous skin routing;
* insufficient profile extent;
* profile branching;
* absent left or right shoulder;
* fine/coarse instability;
* fewer than seven passing stations;
* capacity overflow;
* nonfinite arithmetic.

There is no substitution with:

* two-sided angle alone;
* tight/wide ratio;
* source count;
* support balance;
* existing long-strip or doublet rules;
* a relaxed station count;
* an alternative profile radius.

### Legitimate close parallel bends

LTCO does **not** count nearby parallel lines and does not suppress a bundle.

A hat/channel with three legitimate parallel bends should produce three separate transverse curvature lobes, with a low-curvature web separating adjacent lobes. Each candidate is evaluated independently against the lobe centered on its own line.

The architecture must be retired if the prospective hat/channel case shows a visually and evaluator-valid bend being rejected merely because another parallel bend lies nearby.

When two nominal bends are so close that the observed source surface contains no stable low-curvature separation of at least three profile bins, LTCO will fail closed. That is a known boundary of this certificate, not permission to merge or tune.

### Important unresolved counterexample

A large-radius bend that has a broad but genuinely bounded curvature corridor may be geometrically indistinguishable from a rolled wall under scan-only evidence, particularly when both have long flat shoulders. LTCO hypothesizes that the target corpus’s bend lines possess a sufficiently compact and persistent curvature corridor.

If a fresh broad-roll negative satisfies the frozen certificate, or a legitimate target bend violates it, retire LTCO. Do not adjust the width fraction or shoulder threshold.

### O003-like unsupported localized responses

An unsupported line can still possess a real localized curvature lobe—for example, at a bead, emboss boundary, reconstruction seam, or other formed feature. LTCO is not guaranteed to remove such a response. The V5R3 vent/bead/emboss cases are therefore essential falsifiers.

## 7. Required manual visual signatures

The native audit must inspect every case and specifically verify:

**Localized fold:** (\theta(u)) has two visible plateaus connected by one compact monotone transition; fine and coarse (q(u)) have overlapping lobes; both shoulders are low; the marker passes through the turn centroid.

**Broad smooth roll:** (\theta(u)) changes gradually across most of the profile; (q(u)) is diffuse or approximately flat; one or both shoulder runs are absent, or corridor width exceeds the frozen fraction. Different parallel candidate positions must not acquire distinct valid ownership lobes.

**Close parallel hat/channel:** each legitimate bend has its own lobe and its own centered marker. The intervening web appears as a curvature valley. No rejection may be justified by line count or parallel proximity.

**Partial occlusion:** failed stations must coincide with visible source absence or route ambiguity. A clearly observed continuous fold rejected merely because the sampler failed is an architecture failure.

**Vent, bead, or emboss:** inspect whether the gate mistakes a formed-feature boundary for a bend corridor. A stable but semantically wrong lobe is a true false positive and retires the mechanism if the quantitative gates are violated.

The profile images are causal audit artifacts, not substitutes for the six native-resolution geometry views.

## Research grounding

Multi-scale point-surface work supports using neighborhood scale as part of the feature definition rather than treating one local curvature response as sufficient. Pauly, Keiser, and Gross explicitly formulate point-cloud feature extraction through scale-dependent surface variation and describe characteristic feature scale and improved robustness from multi-scale analysis. ([CGL ETHZ][1])

Integral-invariant geometry provides a second relevant foundation: local PCA or integrated neighborhoods yield scale-dependent curvature-like descriptors, with averaging used to reduce the instability of direct differentiation on noisy scanned geometry. ([Geometrie][2])

Classical ridge/valley extraction defines surface feature lines through extrema and derivatives of principal curvature rather than the mere presence of cylindrical curvature. That supports demanding a localized transverse curvature extremum or corridor ownership before converting a smooth surface response into a line. ([Max Planck Institute][3])

What the literature does **not** establish is the Sherman-specific choice of nine stations, the (25%) shoulder level, the (0.30) station width fraction, the (0.25) candidate median width fraction, or the seven-of-nine consensus. Those are deliberately frozen engineering hypotheses whose validity must come only from the fresh prospective V5R3 falsification.

Disposition: **conditional ordering recommendation only; no execution release**.

The four synthetic positives and a fully GREEN native L-R accuracy result are
not universal prerequisites for an honest consumed-development diagnostic.
They remain binding prerequisites of the *currently written* V5/native protocols.
Neither may be bypassed by calling a failed qualification a diagnostic after it
starts. Any changed next order needs an explicit prospective protocol and
independent PRE, while the original attempt and all its oracles stay terminal
and unchanged.

No candidate, test, oracle, scorer, threshold, resource cap or protocol was
changed. This document alone was written. Review used named source/narrative
documents and current user/parent context; no target imports, tests, experiments,
raw/Gold/control/attempt/archive or active scientific artifacts were accessed.

## What the locked plan actually requires

The [active roadmap](2026-09-07_production_generalization_roadmap.md) distinguishes
offline validation targets from requirements that every engineering iteration
already pass. It asks for connected output evidence with all errors, refusals
and resource costs; correct measurements and later independent physical
qualification remain separate exits.

The [standing mandate](2026-09-09_autonomous_cad_engine_mandate.md), active
sequence item 5, explicitly calls for a frozen independently audited all-case
development execution once interfaces permit an honest result, including many
abstentions. It excludes held-outs, changed truth, external release, hidden
failures and relabelled retries. The latest request to continue until a new
Gold11 F1 reinforces that immediate deliverable; it does not change metric
definitions or guarantee a defined/nonzero score.

The [output-recovery plan](2026-09-10_gold11_output_recovery_execution_plan.md)
requires connected mechanism evidence before the next all-eleven comparison
and prohibits helper-only progress being substituted for that comparison.
It does not require universal CAD support. An independently audited mixed
connected result can be genuine mechanism/failure evidence without satisfying
the original full-positive qualification gate.

However, the current [V5 protocol](2026-09-11_gold11_mesh_development_v5_protocol.md)
explicitly requires all four original connected cases and the native L-R case.
The [native protocol](2026-09-11_two_plane_depth_v1_native_protocol.md) and its
[blocked PRE](2026-09-11_two_plane_depth_v1_native_pre_run_audit.md) explicitly
require a GREEN four-case connected POST before native execution, and all
39 PASS including the original L-R accuracy oracles for native qualification.
Those provisions are real, not optional wording. The default current order is
still blocked if those conditions fail.

A prospective distinction between diagnostic admission and accuracy promotion
can implement the user's existing mandate without lowering roadmap targets.
Record it explicitly in a new versioned gate/protocol or an unstarted draft's
dated amendment, preserve the prior reviewed version/hash and state why the
order changes. This review neither performs nor authorizes that amendment.
Changes to product direction, final acceptance targets or promotion policy
would still require the roadmap's separate authority.

## The terminal failure type determines the next decision

Parent-reported active progress is U-R/U-A required positives, L-R registration
passing but its required measurement oracle failing, and L-A still running.
The exact L-R assertion and complete result have not been inspected here.
This cannot establish whether there was a refusal, an inaccurate prediction,
an integrity defect or an oracle/fixture issue. Do not decide from that partial
summary.

| Independently established terminal finding | Consequence for a new diagnostic |
| --- | --- |
| Explicit conservative refusal, missing usable support or no finite carrier, with correct status and intact custody | May be retained as a failed/abstained case under a new diagnostic gate. No need to manufacture a positive before measuring its development consequence. Original positive oracle remains failed. |
| Finite, correctly bound conditional scan-derived point estimate misses a radius/angle/localization truth limit | This is an accuracy error, not automatically corrupted evidence. It may be scored as an unqualified prediction, retaining every output/error. It cannot be presented as validated measurement, and localization F1 does not clear the failed radius/angle oracle. |
| Broken source/raw-row identity, truth entering inference, nominal substitution represented as scan measurement, fabricated support, invalid/nonfinite endpoint contract, false frame/pose/count/conformance authority, or lost/rewritten artifacts | Blocks the current measurement/scoring contract. Fix the exact integrity defect and independently qualify its boundary before the new physical diagnostic. Merely labelling the final score exploratory cannot repair false provenance or meaning. |
| Killed worker, missing result/footer or incomplete source/custody record | Preserve unknown internal stages and the original negative. A future diagnostic requires a sound bounded execution/retention path; do not treat missing evidence as harmless abstention or infer a cheap completed stage. |
| Fixture/constructor/oracle incompatibility rather than engine truth error | Independently identify the source-level defect. Preserve the original negative; any corrected test or new scope is separately frozen. Do not silently loosen an original oracle or declare the engine qualified. |

A prediction can be wrong while remaining an honest prediction: that is part of
what evaluation measures. Conversely, a known contract violation is not repaired
by allowing it to contribute a numerical FP. The boundary is evidence identity
and declared meaning, not whether the output happens to match Gold.

Do not use the known L-R failure to filter individual Gold11 parts, choose
per-case policies, suppress bad emitted lines or invent a successful subset.
A necessary failure guard must be a general independently reviewed contract
rule, not a truth-derived exception. Preserve the unchanged scorer and its
whole-case refusal semantics.

## What the qualified scorer does and does not assert

The unchanged contact scorer
[`gold11_contact_scoring_v2.py`](../../scripts/gold11_contact_scoring_v2.py)
already emits COMPUTED_DEVELOPMENT_DIAGNOSTIC. Its accepted line is explicitly
a conditional scan-supported contact-sector generator, not physical trim.
Registration is not an accepted whole-part pose; joint configuration and
manufacturing-event identity are unknown. Material side is assumed/unverified,
units/frame compatibility remain provisional, uncertainty uncalibrated and
conformance indeterminate. Structural projection does not independently attest
raw support, native geometry or external custody.

It reuses fixed one-to-one geometric matching on all 11 cases/six families/
71 references. Invalid individual projections retain their case with no
predictions and all its reference FNs. Complete-map/reference or unexpected
matcher failures raise instead of inventing engine outcomes.

Therefore a valid diagnostic does not require all prediction geometry to be
correct in advance. It requires the prediction contract, original bytes,
scoring basis and limitations to be honest. A numeric endpoint F1 is not an
angle/radius validation, physical observed-count accuracy, accepted-pose result,
historical superiority or evidence of unseen-family performance. Known synthetic
measurement errors must remain alongside the score, not disappear behind it.

All-abstain/zero-prediction results may leave precision/F1 undefined under the
unchanged convention. A fresh execution is not a promise of the numeric F1 the
user wants. If that occurs, report the actual undefined result and continue
the mechanism work under the mandate; never replace null by a favorable value.

Retain the following concrete minimum, with exact independently reviewed bytes:

   confirm empty writers, complete the original independent POST and classify
   every failed mandatory oracle. Its original aggregate remains RED if any
   required case fails. No active-artifact peeking, retry or cap change.

   At least a complete bounded native diagnostic of that route is justified
   before spending the physical evaluation; source/mocked constructors alone
   do not execute the native adapter. It may establish a correct refusal rather
   than the original required positive, but must not be called GREEN L-R
   measurement qualification. Earlier native results qualify only their exact
   reviewed source/scope, not this successor by name.
3. **Qualified source/scorer/transport.** Reuse unchanged qualified pure scorer,
   projection and engine-boundary evidence; close any relevant integrity defect.
   Complete the final 85-outcome runner source/mock gate with the exact final
   configuration. Preserve original bytes, all request/binding maps, all-22
   pre-Gold reconstruction, whole-case failure handling, exact 119-file schema,
   256 KiB envelopes and three final inventory phases. No smaller surrogate.
4. **Fixed resources and physical PRE.** Freeze all seven physical time limits
   prospectively using completed work and explicit engineering headroom; fund
   all 22 case opportunities plus scorer, validation and finalization, without
   promising completion. Bind exact consumed input/reference identities,
   unchanged matcher/conventions, fixed arm order/configuration, complete source
   closure and independently accepted PRE/archive before one serial run.
5. **Independent physical POST and narrow report.** Preserve all returned
   predictions, original results, failures, abstentions, per-family results,
   stage/resource evidence and complete custody. Source/receipt/inventory
   failure remains a release veto, not a repaired campaign. Report the fresh
   paired diagnostic and known limitations; do not integrate/promote a change
   merely because it produced a new score.

The native requirement in item 2 is a byte/integrity/resource admission question,
not a demand to fix every scientific error first. A separately frozen native
diagnostic can retain all original geometry oracles and their failures while
evaluating its explicitly separated integrity criteria. This must be specified
*before that new native attempt*, never by reclassifying an existing failed
native qualification afterward. Every original measurement oracle remains
visible and unchanged; no 39-PASS claim is made unless all 39 actually pass.

There is no requirement here for another broad synthetic matrix, universal
native support or unchanged successful-suite reruns. The minimum is the exact
relevant interface/integrity proof, not an unlimited search for all-positive
science. Nor is the source 85-PASS gate by itself a substitute for actual
native-byte or physical evidence.

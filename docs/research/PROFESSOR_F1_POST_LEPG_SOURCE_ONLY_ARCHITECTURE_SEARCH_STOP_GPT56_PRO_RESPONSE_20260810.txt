RULING
LEPG V1 is terminal. No further source-only architecture is authorized.
The visually correct SF01 profile, followed by the rejection of all 13,568 shared-center dual-circle models before open-sector evaluation, is a valid representation-stage false negative. It does not authorize an open-sector correction, altered profile model, changed fixture, rerun, or C006 access. No C006 claim is to be created or consumed.
This ruling is a funding and experimental-design stop, not a theorem that no source-only algorithm could ever detect a bend.
Why another source-only family is no longer scientifically rational
The closed program has now sampled the materially different information classes available in the same static XYZ source:


intrinsic and graph structure: CIIP and GCDF;


spherical-normal rank, Gauss-image trajectories, and ruling coherence: SDRA and PGRR;


opposite-skin, midsurface, focal-center, and twin-radius structure: AMHC and CFAC;


local rigid-motion coherence and extrusion/profile grammar: LSSC and LEPG.


The most plausible remaining constructions are alternative estimators of those same observables, not new causal measurements:


Integral-invariant and varifold methods can provide more robust estimates of curvature or curvature measures, but they still derive differential geometry from the same point support. The primary literature presents them as curvature-estimation machinery, with regularization and scale choices—not as a source of semantic bend ownership. UT Austin Computer Science+1


Hough, RANSAC, quadric, cylinder, or constrained least-squares approaches would fit a different primitive representation to selected points. Lukács, Martin, and Marshall explicitly separate segmentation—deciding which observations belong together—from subsequent primitive fitting. Better cylinder optimization therefore does not supply the missing ownership decision. ORCA


Volumetric signed-distance, medial, offset-surface, or skeleton constructions would primarily re-express AMHC/CFAC’s opposite-skin and center-structure evidence.


A training-free embedding or self-supervised descriptor would still leave its score, model, checkpoint, and selection criterion unidentified under the present no-label/no-feedback contract.


LEPG is particularly informative because the relevant geometry survived translation consensus and profile collapse. The failure occurred while deciding which mathematically plausible representation should count as a bend. Opening another source-only family would therefore continue a search over hand-authored selection rules without gaining the missing empirical variable: which geometrically coherent structures are physical bend objects rather than aliases, fillets, perimeter structures, terminals, holes, rolled features, or other curved manufacturing geometry.
That is also incompatible with a serious family-held-out objective. Domain-generalization research treats model selection as part of the method; algorithms without a justified model-selection strategy are experimentally incomplete. Cross-domain work likewise shows why evidence from one closed geometric world cannot establish transfer to another. OpenReview+1
Smallest necessary capability opening
Open a quarantined family-disjoint semantic-adjudication oracle, while leaving C006 sealed.
This opening does not require releasing CAD, point masks, filenames, expected bend counts, or target-family information to inference. Its minimum form is:


Two precommitted non-C006 development families


Family A: iterative development and model selection.


Family B: one immutable leave-one-family-out validation.


C006 remains the untouched final target.




Hidden evaluator rather than exposed gold
For each frozen submitted axis/object candidate, return only:


TRUE_BEND_OBJECT with an opaque object ID, or a non-bend ownership class;


duplicate-object attribution, so several fragments cannot masquerade as separate discoveries;


TP, FP, and FN;


direction, line-distance, radius, bend-angle, and continuation errors under the already established physical tolerances;


a coarse false-positive class such as PERIMETER_OR_TERMINAL, HOLE, NONBEND_CURVED_FEATURE, DUPLICATE_ALIAS, or UNMATCHED.


Do not expose gold coordinates, CAD geometry, expected object count, or correspondence tables to the detector.


Strict separation


Family A feedback may be used to determine which causal evidence actually predicts bend ownership.


All constants, architecture, and selection rules are then frozen.


Family B is executed once.


C006 may be considered only after a successful Family B transfer result under a separately locked authorization.




This is the smallest opening because it adds only the information currently absent—semantic object ownership across families—while retaining zero target leakage and preserving C006 as a genuine one-shot evaluation. A single annotated part would not suffice: it would permit part-specific explanation but provide no family-transfer evidence.
Closure
Closed and unauthorized:


LEPG open-sector or profile-model correction;


LEPG rerun or fixture alteration;


C006 opening under LEPG;


retuning, renaming, fusing, or routing through SDRA, AMHC, CFAC, LSSC, LEPG, PGRR, CIIP, or GCDF;


another static source-only architecture selected only from synthetic-fixture behavior.

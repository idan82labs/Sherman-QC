LINEAR_EXTRUSION_PROFILE_GRAMMAR_V1
Single causal hypothesis
A genuine straight sheet-metal bend is a partial linear extrusion: translation along the bend axis reproduces a perpendicular two-dimensional profile containing two concentric open circular arcs, one per skin, with a tangent straight continuation at both ends of both arcs. The bend axis is the line through the shared profile center parallel to the translation direction.
A flat plate lacks the arcs. A single skin lacks the second offset arc. A complete cylinder lacks the open-sector and tangent-continuation grammar. A through-hole lacks the required axial span or double open profile. A terminal wall lacks circular-sector support.
The established basis is the generalized-cylinder and extrusion representation of shape by a swept cross-section, translational-symmetry extraction of such parts from unorganized point clouds, and the generation of approximate Euclidean-symmetry candidates from local correspondences followed by explicit spatial verification.

scan cases had no existing member covering the complete ownership component.

only for that structural failure. These tests qualify the planner's set,

Exactly `tests/test_cad_guided_raw_support_union_v1.py` is selected: 28 test
functions and 93 prospective parametrized outcomes, independently reconciled

registration, numerical fitting, Gold, retained prediction, email or held-out
input is selected. Fixtures are independently invented coordinate labels; the
largest materialized fixture has 32 XYZ rows, four records and 30 memberships.
The million-row/four-million-membership checks use scalar accounting only.

The decision is PASS only if all 93 outcomes pass, with no missing, extra,
failed, skipped, xfailed, xpassed, interrupted or collection-error outcome;
the worker exits zero; all resource, exact source-custody, writer-quiescence
and terminal publication gates pass. A missing result is failure, not a pass.
Retain the whole attempt and every failure. Do not rerun the unchanged attempt
or change its limits. A successor needs a documented mechanism or harness
change, a separately frozen protocol and independent PRE.

PASS permits consideration of separately gated numerical/wrapper work only.
It does not establish physical bend identity, valid fits, original-byte or
association-filter lineage, finite coverage, observed counts, uncertainty,
physical F1, unseen-family accuracy, integration or factory-floor release.

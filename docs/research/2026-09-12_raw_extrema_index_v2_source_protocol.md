## Fixed accounting and prospective decision

Common abstract work is cumulative `coordinate_visits` plus 37 for each
actually executed old V1 world bound. The derivation is six sign comparisons,
six coordinate selections, two times (nine staged arithmetic plus two zero
handling steps), and three finite/order checks. Active primitive queries
already contribute 390 steps plus packing; disabled shear uses the same old
world-bound supplement. The full counter vectors and formula are retained.
This is not complete hardware-operation accounting or a new physical scorer.

For 32,768 rows, active construction alone adds 2,008,832 primitive leaf steps
plus merging, chooser and packing; leaf savings alone cannot establish gain.
The CPU observation is instrumented process CPU around each constructor and
each query, excluding oracle and report work, with V1 preceding V2. No repeat,
best-of timing or post-result tolerance is authorized by this protocol.

Integrate this mechanism only if ALL hold:

1. All sixteen ordinary test items pass, including exact complete results and
   original IDs on every query in all four efficacy populations.
2. The full twelve-query common work is strictly lower than V1 on BOTH
   `resolved_unique_extrema` AND `folded_three_planes`.
3. The sum of construction-plus-query CPU seconds over ALL four V2 cases is
   no larger than the corresponding V1 sum; flat/plateau overheads count.
4. Exact source/runtime/publication custody and independent POST are accepted.

Otherwise retain the terminal negative result and pursue a documented new
mechanism/version. Do not reinterpret lower leaf counts, a favorable prefix,
one easy population or correctness alone as passing this decision. Even a
positive standalone result requires an audited connected path and separately
frozen physical successor before another Gold11 run. No numeric F1 is produced

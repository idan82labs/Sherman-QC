# gb_4cb0f9 marker A
Full scan of a long (~680 mm) bent profile, t~1.5: top 1/2, vertical wall 5/6, sloped face 3/4; both ends closed by end caps (7/9 at one end, 8/18 at the other) made of folded flaps with diagonal mitre seams. 7 bends.
- B1 1|5: top to wall, 90.8 deg, 678 mm full length.
- B2 1|4: top to sloped face, 47.3 deg. The auto extent stopped at t 287 because face 4 is notched near end 7. Extended to end cap to end cap (t -323..355.6, the intersection with the end-cap planes); fold support 1.0.
- B3 1|7, B4 5|7, B5 4|7: the end-cap flaps at end 7, folded from the top, the wall and the sloped face. Each has a clean formed outer radius (apex offset ~1.2 mm = nominal).
- B6 1|8, B7 4|8: the end-cap flaps at end 8 from the top and the sloped face. Formed radius.
Rejected: 5|8 (wall to end cap 8). The section shows two separate plate edges with a gap/seam, no continuous arc (apex offset 2.9 against the nominal 1.2), so it is an open or welded corner, not a bend. The diagonal mitre seams on the end caps are not bends. Small patches 10-25 are inner-skin fragments, edges and holes.
Doubts: the 3-flap end cap at end 7 against the 2-flap one at end 8 is unusual. I checked each section. B4 (5|7) has a noisy inner skin (possible flap overlap), but its outer arc is clean.

1. Research grounding
Published results

Pauly, Keiser, and Gross showed that covariance eigenvalues of local point neighborhoods can provide a surface-variation measure, and that evaluating such a measure at multiple neighborhood sizes can improve line-feature extraction under noise. Their method, however, proceeds through thresholding, graph construction, and active contours, and they explicitly describe parameter adjustment and user feedback as part of the workflow. 
CGL ETHZ

Pottmann, Wallner, Huang, and Yang established that integrated local-neighborhood descriptors can have useful robustness and multiscale behavior compared with direct differential estimates. Their analysis supports using scale behavior itself as geometric evidence, rather than treating one neighborhood radius as authoritative. 
UT Austin Computer Science

Mérigot, Ovsjanikov, and Guibas showed that covariance-based integral measures can recover curvature information and encode the directions and angles of sharp features on piecewise-smooth point-sampled surfaces, with robustness results for their particular Voronoi-covariance construction. 
PubMed

Local-neighborhood asymmetry has separately been used to detect generic point-cloud edges, including workpiece edges, but that line of work detects missing support at ordinary boundaries and requires dataset-dependent neighborhood and threshold choices. That is precisely why SDRA does not use centroid displacement or first-moment asymmetry as its operative signal. 
arXiv

5. Roles and independence
Ordinary producer

Exact integer-distance k-nearest-neighbor search using a deterministic balanced kd-tree with exact branch-and-bound completion.

Block size: 4,096 anchors.

Fixed-point direction moments, eigenvalues, model fits, candidate mask, codimension audit, stage ledger, and visuals.

No source coordinates serialized.

Before source canonical sorting, order raw point records by

SHA256("SDRA_V1_PERM"∥source SHA∥raw IEEE-754 XYZ bytes).

Ties use raw XYZ bytes. Exact duplicate records later trigger the source-contract wall.

It then runs the ordinary producer unchanged. Its semantic hash and terminal token must exactly equal the ordinary role.

Materially independent validator

The validator must:

be a separate executable or module tree;

import no producer geometry, neighbor, eigensolver, model-fit, candidate, or renderer-state code;

rebuild the source from raw bytes;

use a Morton-cell exact search with cell-distance lower bounds rather than the producer kd-tree;

use a separately implemented symmetric 3×3 eigensolver;

independently enumerate the nonnegative model active sets;

recompute every quantized per-point scalar, count, percentile, terminal condition, and semantic hash;

independently verify the stage-state ledger and visual manifest.

Only canonical encoding/hash helpers may be shared. All three role semantic hashes and all three terminal decisions must agree.

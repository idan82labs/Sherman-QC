2. Sherman-specific hypothesis

The hypothesis under test is:

Repeated intrinsic shortest paths crossing a physical bend will have positive ambient chord deficit whose fixed-scale local maximum backprojects to a coherent, approximately line-like source-space locus; a hinge rotation fitted on one deterministic half of those paths will reduce the deficit on the held-out half.

There are three immediate falsifiers:

graph discretization or scan noise produces nonselective chord deficit almost everywhere;

deficit exists but does not localize into coherent line support;

a line-like concentration exists but a held-out hinge intervention does not explain it.

Any of those valid outcomes stops this family. There is no threshold rescue.

3. Why this is causally distinct

The operative observable is a comparison between intrinsic path length and ambient endpoint distance. It does not require:

projective poles, Gauss-map bands, great circles, angular arcs, or ruled preimages;

local plane-fragment objects, parent fragments, direct or cross-parent rungs;

pointwise tensor maxima;

paired local wedges or paired transition strips;

transition-mode births or clustering;

cubical occupancy, filtrations, persistence, merge witnesses, or alternate voxel phases;

changes to target tubes, pair support, side gates, or certificates.

The optional tangent estimate disappears after the surface graph freezes. No normal value participates in path scoring, voting, candidate formation, or the causal result.

The PGRR near-misses specifically favor this test. PGRR generated more than ten million pole incidences but fragmented them into 192,396 components, with 98.18% failing member count. GCDF instead integrates evidence along source-space paths and only then backprojects it. A smooth source fragment can therefore accumulate support even if its normals split across angular groups or never form a sufficiently long great-circle component. Conversely, a globally smooth curved panel may also create chord excess; the localization and held-out unfolding walls are intended to kill that confound.

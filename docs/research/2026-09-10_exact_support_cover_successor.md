## Independently reviewed mechanism; implementation still unexecuted

Add a separately versioned support-cover aggregation path; do not edit V1's
already-tested blanket-veto behavior or silently change V12 results.

1. On nonfatal paths preserve and validate the complete original context result, raw-row/source
   bindings, exclusions, member roles and all nominal/context associations.
   A late fatal exception or total-report overflow clears the whole new report,
   including nested evidence; do not claim unconditional nested retention.
   Terminal experiment records and the frozen predecessor remain separate.
2. Build bounded raw-support overlap components. Arc/flange conflict remains a
   hard veto. Each member must map its two nonempty flange supports uniquely
   into an existing whole-component cover's two roles. This direct common-cover
   mapping establishes global permutation consistency without assuming every
   pair independently shares flange rows. Incompatible fit policies cannot be pooled.
3. Require one existing member whose aligned raw arc/flange sets cover every
   member, equivalently equal the entire component's three role unions. No
   existing cover means abstain; do not choose a favorable subset or construct
   an unobserved union. Exact raw IDs and roles decide this, not fit quality,
   CAD identity, truth, expected bend count or parameter-distance thresholds.
4. Refit the entire cover's raw positions with the unchanged scan-only free-
   circle/flange/finite-support rules. Preserve every component failure and
   member association. Do not resurrect erased finite-support status by inference.
5. Publish at most one conditional geometric support carrier per qualified
   cover group. Count, joint pose, manufacturing-event identity, uncertainty
   intervals, material side and physical trim remain unknown/unverified.
   Member contexts assert their own support only; the cover is supported by its
   witness context, not falsely by every member's crop or exclusion filter.

Independent math review supports the existing-cover set proof: because the
cover itself belongs to the component, subset inclusion implies exact union
equality. One fresh full-cover model is a common sample-model witness for its
subsets; it is not a claim that each member was independently refit or that
disconnected/noisy samples identify one physical surface. Member finite refusals
remain visible, but only cover finite-support gates determine carrier eligibility.

The additive helper and measurement wrapper are now written, with independent

This is a narrow conservative successor, not universal bend-instance grouping.

## Research context and nonclaims

[Toldo and Fusiello, J-Linkage, ECCV2008](https://fusiello.github.io/papers/eccv08.pdf)
use model-preference overlap and a common-model condition to form point clusters,
then fit final models. This motivates distinguishing compatible alternative
supports from competing physical instances. The proposed exact existing-support
cover is not J-Linkage: it adopts neither random sampling, greedy Jaccard merges,
outlier removal nor that paper's experimental model-count selection. The source
does not prove our grouping or physical-event identity; our explicit conditional
model and adversarial checks must supply the relevant engineering evidence.

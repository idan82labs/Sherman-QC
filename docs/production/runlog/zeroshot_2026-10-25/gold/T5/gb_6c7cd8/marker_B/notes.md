# gb_6c7cd8, marker B

Small U bracket, about 105 x 43 mm, t about 1 mm. It has a web (1/2) and two legs about 13.5 mm tall (outer skins 3 and 6). Each leg has three narrow tabs, about 8 mm wide, that are bent 90 deg inward to form mounting feet with holes. The scan is full.

Bends (8):
- B1, patches 1 to 3, and B2, patches 1 to 6: web to leg, about 90 deg. Each fold is broken by two reliefs at the feet (gaps of about 5 mm), so each counts as 1 line. Fold support is 0.9.
- B3 to B5: leg 3 to its three feet.
- B6 to B8: leg 6 to its three feet.
- How the feet were fitted: the feet are not segmented, so each uses `plane_from_points` seeded on the foot underside (the outer skin of the leg-to-foot bend), with r 4.5 and tol 0.6.
- Each foot line is about 6.5 mm long on a tab about 8 mm wide (at least 80 %), at about 90 deg, with certain confidence.

Rejected: the hole walls in the feet and the relief notches in the web.

Doubts: I counted each tab fold as its own line, because the tabs are separate plates rather than one fold split by a window. If the adjudicator merges collinear tab folds, the per-leg tab lines are collinear (same t lines, three per leg).

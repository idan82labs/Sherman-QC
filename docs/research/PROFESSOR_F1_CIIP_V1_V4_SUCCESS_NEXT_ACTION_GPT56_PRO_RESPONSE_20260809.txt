2. Authorized next tranche
CIIP_V1_FROZEN_TARGET_ATTRIBUTION_PROBE_V1
Scientific question

Can the already-produced CIIP-V1 H0 merge structure generate at least one deterministic, geometrically local, non-baseline witness that reaches a frozen F1 target, advances its existing side gate, and is necessary for a novel certificate?

This is the smallest useful next move because it tests the mechanism against the actual frozen F1 representation without opening gold. If CIIP cannot affect even one target under a fixed mapping, the family has reached an immediate empirical wall and should be retired before labels, alternate scans, or tuning are considered.

This is an ordinary execution tranche, not another preflight or approval packet.

5. Exact CIIP witness construction

The mapping must use all finite canonical H0 merge events. No persistence cutoff is permitted.

For every finite H0 interval or equivalent canonical merge record:

Identify the component that dies under the locked elder/tie-breaking rule.

Identify the surviving component into which it merges.

Identify the canonical death cell responsible for the merge.

Immediately before insertion of that death cell, identify the incident cells belonging to the dying and surviving components.

On each side, select the representative incident cell using the existing canonical cell ordering. If more than one cell is eligible, select the lexicographically first one. No geometric score may replace this rule.

Convert the two representatives and the death cell into physical coordinates using the locked cubical grid transform.

The local witness support is the union of:

the dying-side representative cell;

the death cell;

the surviving-side representative cell.

The associated candidate rung is the undirected segment between the two representative-cell barycentres, carrying the death cell as its mandatory support witness.

This is a project-specific engineering interpretation of an H0 merge event. Persistent-homology literature establishes the birth/merge structure; it does not establish that this three-cell witness corresponds to a manufacturing defect.

Fail-closed localization condition

A merge row is non-localizable only when the canonical complex cannot reconstruct two distinct predecessor components incident to the death cell.

Such rows must be counted and retained in an exception table. They must not be silently repaired, expanded into a neighborhood search, or assigned substitute representatives.

6. Mapping witnesses to frozen targets

A CIIP witness reaches a target only when the existing frozen predicates establish all of the following:

one representative belongs to the target’s first frozen side region;

the other representative belongs to its second frozen side region, in either order;

the death cell belongs to the target’s existing frozen target tube or support predicate;

the witness and target are source-cross-bound;

the rung is accepted by all pre-existing structural validity checks.

No new distance, angle, overlap, persistence, or ranking threshold may be introduced.

Every valid witness-target relationship is retained. There is no “best witness” selection.

The added rung must be tagged:

origin_family = CIIP_V1
origin_witness_id = <witness_id>
origin_target_id = <target_id>

Its identifier must be a SHA-256 over the canonical serialization of the source hash, target ID, witness ID, endpoint cells, death cell, and construction version.

7. Required paired evaluation

The probe must evaluate exactly two graph states:

A. Augmented state

The frozen baseline plus all valid CIIP-V1 rungs.

B. CIIP ablation state

Byte-identical to the augmented state except that every edge with:

origin_family = CIIP_V1

is removed.

No baseline edge, mode, fragment, component, target, side gate, or evaluator state may differ between A and B.

A certificate is CIIP-attributable only when:

it exists in the augmented state;

it does not exist in the CIIP ablation state;

its derivation contains at least one CIIP witness rung;

the corresponding target’s side-gate state changes from not-advanced in B to advanced in A.

A certificate is novel only when it is not equivalent to any frozen baseline certificate under the already-locked equivalence predicate. That predicate may not be widened, narrowed, or replaced.

12. How this result changes the next decision

A zero result retires CIIP-V1 without opening gold.

A positive result justifies preserving CIIP-V1 and moving to the smallest sealed task-relevance evaluation. At that point, continued absolute closure of all truth makes an actual F1-generalization conclusion impossible: internal agreement, persistence, invariance, and graph attribution cannot reveal whether the affected targets are correct. The eventual smallest scientifically necessary opening would be a read-only, blinded evaluation of the frozen CIIP intervention on a predeclared held-out truth set, with no tuning and no feedback until the complete result is committed.

That truth opening is not authorized by this ruling. The authorized action now is solely:

CIIP_V1_FROZEN_TARGET_ATTRIBUTION_PROBE_V1.

records, exact supported scalar forms, both lanes, array descriptors and original
triangle-to-raw unions before publication. Both lanes share one owned selector.

are eligible. Unknown/noncap incidence and unreferenced raw rows are retained;
coincident coordinates do not merge original identities. Rejected records are
validated but not promoted to qualified cones. These are conditional geometric
roles, not native manufacturing truth or an unconditional physical cap proof.

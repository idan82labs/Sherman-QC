## Engineering reason and question

V8 completed on all 22 slots with zero emitted supports: 12 completed
prefilters without a passing start, 4 CPU terminations inside surface queries,
2 proposal membership refusals, 1 residual rejection at the 1.0 mm gate and 3
admitted contexts whose 43 native windows were all refused by the local flange
gates. V9 changes four things in the engine and its declared policies, plus the
consumer-chain bindings those changes require, and nothing else:

- initializer V15 adds a bounded, additive two-of-three triplet completion lane
  (third CAD face re-assignment along the passing pair's intersection
  direction, candidate-specific normal sign, every selected child published
  before any fallible work, rescreened and requeried on all three supports with
  the unchanged finite prefilter, a separate declared budget envelope whose
  inactive inherited fields are declared, a lane byte cap re-applied after the
  last mutation, main-lane strata scheduled first, and a NON-exhaustive
  |shift|-then-face-id ranking that claims no uniqueness or coverage);
- measurement kernel V2 publishes both-flange/both-branch diagnostics for every
  window (with explicit completed/failed phases retained on refusals and on
  numerical failures) and applies a declared geometric layer selection: every
  qualifying gap of the material-side coordinate is enumerated; exactly one
  qualifying split whose two groups pass the scan-derived pair compatibility
  (per-group planarity/rank, parallel normals, offset within the declared gap
  window, projected bounding-interval overlap — an explicit proxy, not a
  material footprint) keeps the SMALLER-coordinate group as
  `TWO_LAYER_INNER_SELECTED_BY_MATERIAL_SIDE_UNVERIFIED`; zero splits,
  two or more splits, or an incompatible pair keep ALL rows with an unresolved
  identity (abstention, never selection by fit quality); the enabled arc filter
  keeps rows on the axis side of both fitted planes; disabled selection is the
  V1 control and supplies no material-side evidence. NO present status is
  inner-face-claim eligible (`INNER_FACE_ELIGIBLE_STATUSES` is empty): the
  two-layer selection is retained as a side HYPOTHESIS in the status and layer
  diagnostics, and every measured window is a GENERIC conditional contact line
  (scored as such) with no nominal inner-radius deviation and no inner-face
  role. All sample-model gate forms are unchanged;
- the measurement policy becomes δ = 0.03 mm / e = 0.04 mm, derived from the
  nominal r 0.78–0.80 mm / 90° geometry of every V8 native window and the
  flat-witness inequality under the exact-nominal clean model (the frozen
  0.05 / 0.075 pair is infeasible for that idealized geometry; diagnosis Part
  E). This is one defensible prospective development candidate, not a unique
  or calibrated choice: the continuous half-width headroom is only ~0.011–0.013
  mm, the finite three-column headroom ~0.0004 mm, so sampling phase and noise
  can still refuse windows (retained negatives). Coupled effects: tighter plane
  and circle residual gates, an eased minor-spread gate (0.5 → 0.3 mm), eased
  flat-alternative rejection and more near-contact arc rows admitted;
- the finite-support instance policy `support.max_angular_gap_deg` becomes 30°
  (V8: 5°), a prospective conditional sample-support (inference coverage)
  policy: on the connected synthetic fixture (five nominal angular columns at
  22.5° on r 0.8 mm / 90°, the ideal e 0.04 exclusion keeping three) the frozen
  5° per-slab gap refuses the support and 30° admits it. This is fixture and
  nominal-arithmetic evidence only, not proof that every physical carrier
  fails 5°, not phase-robust (a half-step lattice phase leaves two columns), a
  chosen development margin rather than a unique or calibrated threshold, and
  no guarantee of a prediction or improved F1. Support abstentions and the
  generic sample-supported interpretation are preserved; max axial gap 4 mm,
  minimum sweep 20° and the 3-row minima are unchanged.

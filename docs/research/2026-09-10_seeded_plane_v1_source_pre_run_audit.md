## Mathematical review of the standalone proposal lane

No blocking mathematical or quantifier error was found in the stated narrow
source-only claim. The relevant distinction is a heuristic region proposal
followed by unchanged, conditional all-row qualification; it is not a confidence
interval for seed-plane orientation or a scan-complete plane detector.

1. The input is an owned snapshot of positions, original integer triangle row
   indices, and stipulated per-row position-error bounds in millimetres. Unchanged
   V5 validation checks shapes, scalar types, finite values, coordinate/range
   limits, valid distinct triangle indices, and nondegeneracy. The owned copy is
   revalidated against index aliasing. Shared-edge topology, not spatial proximity
   or CAD labels, determines adjacency. A nonmanifold shared-edge fan is refused.

2. The finite seed schedule is frozen before region or fit outcomes. At most 32
   original triangles are selected: largest area first, then alternating observed
   unoriented-normal novelty and centroid spread, with area and original triangle
   ID tie breaking. These are finite, ordering-sensitive proposal priorities.
   Omitting a possible seed is disclosed, and rejection cannot refill the schedule.
   Complete selected-policy evaluation is not exhaustive surface coverage.

3. A seed fixes one observed triangle normal and anchor for the entire region
   proposal. For row `i`, inclusion uses
   `abs((p_i - anchor) dot normal) <= epsilon_i + max(seed epsilon) + guard`.
   At uniform epsilon 0.05 mm this is a 0.10 mm proposal slab, plus a floating-point
   engineering allowance. Seed-normal error is explicitly not enclosed by it.
   The observed triangle-normal angle test is likewise a heuristic. There is no
   fit during growth and no chain of progressively turning neighbor normals.

4. All three original triangle vertices must pass the fixed slab. The region is
   exactly the seed-containing component of the eligible induced original
   shared-edge graph. A larger unrelated component cannot replace it. The region
   remains an original triangle-ID union with its holes and separate components;
   no filled polygon, coordinate welding, or inferred connection is introduced.

5. Exactly equal triangle-ID regions are deduplicated only after exact equality
   is checked, not from a digest alone. Original raw-ID support is the union of
   all referenced rows, including distinct IDs at identical coordinates. A unique
   region receives one unchanged V5 full-support fit using the original error
   bounds. No outlier trimming or proposal-slab enlargement enters qualification.

6. At the fitted fixed normal, the all-row offset witness is the intersection of
   row intervals: lower endpoint `max(projection_i - epsilon_i)`, upper endpoint
   `min(projection_i + epsilon_i)`. V5 also imposes its unchanged conditional normal
   cone/observability gate. That cone is conditional on the stipulated point-error
   model and numerical assumptions, not calibrated statistical confidence. More
   rows do not introduce an unjustified independent-noise `1/sqrt(N)` reduction.
   Engineering floating allowances are not directed-rounded interval arithmetic
   or proof of latent physical flatness.

7. Complete results retain the dispositions of every scheduled seed, including
   rejected and duplicate supports. An ordinary late exception, codec failure, or
   exceeded logical cap invalidates all publishable seed/region/proposal records;
   it does not preserve a favorable prefix. Attempted/discarded work and the
   already frozen schedule may remain as diagnostic evidence. A zero-seed policy
   is disclosed as complete but nonexhaustive, never physical absence.

The method is related only at the proposal-design level to fixed seed-face region
growing described in the official
[CGAL Shape Detection manual](https://doc.cgal.org/latest/Shape_detection/index.html).
This code is not the CGAL implementation and its conditional V5 qualification is
not established by that analogy. No algorithm-equivalence or research-theorem
claim is needed for the present bounded fixture gate.

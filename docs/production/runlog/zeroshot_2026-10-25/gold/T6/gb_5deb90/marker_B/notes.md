# gb_5deb90 marker B

Flat plate with a stepped bracket leg, t ~1.5 mm. 4 bends, all ~90 deg, each 68-69 mm.

| line | patches | what |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | 2-10 | plate 1/2 down to short wall 9/10 (~7 mm step) |
| B2 | 9-12 | short wall 9/10 to narrow ledge 11/12 |
| B3 | 11-3 | ledge 11/12 down to wall 3/4 (~17 mm) |
| B4 | 4-[5,6] | wall 3/4 to the two foot tabs 5/8 and 6/7 |

B4: the feet are coplanar. Between them the bottom of wall 3/4 has a ~37 mm window, so the fold exists only at the two feet. Following the 'split by windows or reliefs = 1 line' rule, it is marked as one line over both feet (fold support 0.46; the gap is the window; uncertain on coverage).
B1 + B2 form a joggle-like step (plate to ledge), which I counted as 2 folds; both are ~90 deg formed folds.
Rejected: the square slots and holes in the plate; patch 13 (a thickness edge of the feet).
Doubts: B4 could be argued as 2 separate tab folds (2 lines of ~15 mm each) if the adjudicator does not treat the wall window as a relief.

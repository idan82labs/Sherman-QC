Immediate implementation, mechanism lock, one frozen sequential source-free execution, and the conditional one-scan prospective action below are authorized without another architecture-review round.

A formal STOP is premature. V33 discarded an observable that remains present in the source: the coupling between ordered transverse position and projective surface-normal direction. SN06 demonstrates failure of the marginalization, not nonidentifiability from the scan.

1. Architecture and causal distinction

Architecture token: V34_ORDERED_PROJECTIVE_GAUSS_TRANSPORT_V1
Primitive: TRANSVERSE_MONOTONE_GAUSS_MEASURE_V1
Short name: OPGT_V1

V33 represented a candidate by fan populations, masses, widths, coverage, and correlations. Those statistics are nearly invariant to rearranging the normal populations across the cross-section. Consequently, 0 → 70 → 35 → 90 can have essentially the same fan moments as 0 → 35 → 70 → 90.

V34 instead retains the joint measure

μ(u,[n]),

where u is ordered transverse position and [n] is an unoriented, projective normal. It accepts only when that measure supports:

two distinct, internally coherent tail-normal populations;

a materially observed intermediate transition;

axial stationarity rather than axial twisting;

one sub-π, one-signed projective-normal progression across u.

This is not a V33 threshold addition. No V33 fan span, fill, phase group, density, width, correlation, coverage, radial-run metric, score, or verdict enters V34.

It is also not V32 reconstructed. V34 has:

no mesh graph;

no adjacency;

no edge or dihedral quantity;

no harmonic potential;

no current field;

no stations or station masks;

no phase-origin solve.

Triangle boundaries are used only to integrate surface area into fixed spatial cells. A triangulation diagonal is never an observable.

2. Research basis versus Sherman hypothesis

For an ideal developable ruled patch, the Gauss map is constant along a ruling. That supports pooling evidence along a candidate bend axis while preserving the ordered variation transverse to it. 
arXiv

Area-integrated geometric quantities are preferable here to derivatives or edge curvature: integration has established robustness advantages against noise and discretization artifacts. 
UT Austin Computer Science

Representing surface orientation projectively is also established machinery. Varifold representations were introduced specifically to encode nonoriented curves and surfaces without requiring globally consistent normal signs. 
SIAM

The following is the Sherman-specific hypothesis, not a published theorem:

Within the frozen V16 candidate class, a recoverable single material bend has two stable tail normals joined by a source-observed, sub-180-degree, one-signed transverse normal progression. A bead, return, opening, disconnected miter, axial twist, or other alias will violate tail separation, transition support, axial coherence, or monotone ordering.

That hypothesis is narrow, source-observable, and directly falsifiable. It does not claim that all possible formed-sheet features are distinguishable by normal order.

It runs only on the frozen V16 rejected-candidate side stream.

It never deletes, moves, rescales, refits, or rescores a V16 legacy-positive line.

A V34 pass emits the original V16 candidate line geometry.

At most one V34 record is emitted per source candidate.

The frozen legacy-first sidecar insertion and priority convention is retained.

V16 legacy candidates are supplied first; V34 passes follow in ascending source-candidate ID.

There is no internal suppression, clustering, merging, or secondary ranking.

The unchanged ordinary NMS is invoked exactly once.

An overlapping V34 rescue must lose to the legacy candidate under the frozen legacy-first convention. NMS is allowed to remove a V34 duplicate but may not alter which legacy lines survive.

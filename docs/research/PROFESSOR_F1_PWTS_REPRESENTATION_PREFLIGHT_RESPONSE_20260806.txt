RSS must cover the entire process tree from initial byte parsing through canonical serialization and final file flush. A cgroup or equivalent hard process-tree limiter is required; inability to obtain full-lifetime accounting is a failure, not permission to fall back to a partial worker sample.

Any structural cap is fail-before-truncation. No top-k clipping, reservoir rescue, compact substitution, or omitted tail is permitted.

10. Mandatory tamper regressions

The validator/tamper suite must operate on copied real preflight evidence, not synthetic scan geometry. It must reject all of the following:

One raw input byte changed while the old raw SHA remains asserted.

Equal-count exchange of two case-token/raw-SHA bindings.

Equal-count sampled-point-ID substitution with forged counts and digest fields.

Any numerical configuration change, even when accompanied by a newly computed but unauthorized configuration hash.

Removal of one seed row with aggregate counts and runner digests adjusted.

Mutation of one trace-node point ID while retaining trace-node count.

Compensating left/right membership exchanges that preserve both side counts.

A certificate changed to valid=true while any recomputed gate is false.

A missing deletion arm, forged deletion result, or asserted destruction without the corresponding raw-ID deletion evidence.

Row reordering, duplicate-and-delete attacks, or noncanonical side/arm ordering while counts remain unchanged.

Injection of raw coordinates, source_points, sampled arrays, or source_points_serialized=true.

Missing, shortened, or forged resource accounting, including a measured cap violation reported as passing.

A forged order-permutation equivalence Boolean with differing semantic table digests.

A validator importing or calling the runner’s semantic implementation.

Each regression must be independently demonstrated to fail closed. Acceptance of any tampered artifact invalidates the real preflight.

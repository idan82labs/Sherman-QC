## Stipulated position bound and prospective mathematical decision

independently designed small noisy-array gate and exceeding the new `.001 mm`

explicit simulator assumption, **not scanner calibration and not a frozen noise
parameter for future Gold/physical runs**. The declaration is bound by a full-row
epsilon-array SHA-256 at registration publication. It is not passed to measurement
as a metrological uncertainty estimate.

In the predecessor V6 protocol, before its first execution/freeze, the initial
`.05 mm` invented-case proposal was rejected by source

`alpha=asin(sqrt(.01/.1066667))≈17.83°`, exceeding the unchanged 10° proposal cap.
At `.005 mm`, the same calculation gives approximately `1.75°`. This prospective
choice supplies a valid bound for the known invented perturbation; it does not
relax the geometric cone cap or claim all real scanner caps will be discovered.

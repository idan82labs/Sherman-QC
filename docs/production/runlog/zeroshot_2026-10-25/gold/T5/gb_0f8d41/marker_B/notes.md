# gb_0f8d41, marker B

Long slotted channel, about 276 x 14 mm, t about 1.2 mm. The scan is full.

The web (1/2) has a long centre slot. Two legs, about 9 mm each, run down the long sides. Each leg is split by reliefs into 5 coplanar segments:
- side A: 4/5, 22/23, 9/11, 18/20 and 12/15;
- side B: 6/7, 19/24, 10/13, 17/21 and 14/16.

One edge of the centre slot carries a lip of about 3 mm (3/8), bent to the side opposite the legs. In the right view this is the T stem.

Bends (3):
- B1, web 1 to side-A outer segments [5,23,11,20,15]: about 86 deg, 255 mm.
  - The face-on view shows a row of small windows along this fold, as well as the reliefs, so fold support is only 0.4 (regular 9 mm gaps).
  - Under the rule that a fold split by windows or reliefs between the same two plates is 1 line, I kept it as 1 full-length line.
- B2, web 1 to side-B outer segments [7,19,10,21,14]: about 92 deg, 267 mm. Fold support is 1.0 (small relief gaps only).
- B3, web 2 to lip face 3: about 83 deg, 255 mm. Fold support is 1.0.

Rejected:
- 25/26: the flat web end beyond the legs. It is parallel to the web, offset under 1 mm, so it is not a joggle.
- The slot and hole walls.

Doubts: B1's low fold support comes from the windows along the fold. The adjudicator may want to confirm the 1-line treatment.

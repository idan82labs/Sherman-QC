2. RESEARCH BASIS
V27: established machinery

The general operation behind V27 is well established. Point-cloud line extraction methods commonly retain a supporting surface region and then consolidate fragmented or adjacent line segments. Lin et al. represent a 3D line together with its attached half-plane support, emphasizing that the surrounding geometric support helps make a line estimate reliable rather than treating coordinates alone as decisive. 
University of Waterloo

Lu, Liu, and Li explicitly include a postprocessing stage that merges nearby 3D line segments using orientation, transverse distance, overlap, and axial-gap conditions. Importantly, they warn that fitting an entirely new line can move the result into a location where no observed line exists; their implementation therefore extends an observed line rather than freely refitting the union. That warning is directly relevant to V27’s PCA replacement and makes retained member provenance and native visual review mandatory. 
arXiv

Established: fragmented, collinear, spatially adjacent line observations may legitimately be reconciled into one geometric line instance.

Sherman-specific: the exact 2° / 6 mm / 20 mm / 1.25× predicate, connected-component transitivity, PCA replacement, and its suitability for Sherman scan families. The literature does not validate those constants or establish that every qualifying component is one physical line.

V28: established machinery

Structural regularity has long been used to stabilize incomplete geometry. GlobFit fits primitives to noisy and incomplete point sets while enforcing global relations such as parallelism, orientation, placement, and equality. Its authors also show that poor data can prevent recovery of all relations, leaving only a partially correct reconstruction. 
Stanford Graphics

Pauly et al. show that repeated geometric structures can be recovered in the presence of noise, outliers, and missing elements, and that the recovered pattern can serve as a prior for reconstructing missing geometry. 
Geometrie
 Mitra, Guibas, and Pauly likewise separate structural hypothesis generation from spatial verification: agreement in transformation space is insufficient until the corresponding surface regions are checked for spatial consistency. 
U of T Computer Science

Established: incomplete man-made geometry may be regularized or completed using repeated parallel, symmetric, or relational structure, provided the proposed relationship is verified against observed surface support.

Sherman-specific: the assertion that one close pair, one distant parallel lane, and one unused fragmented candidate identify the missing member of a bilateral sheet-metal return. No cited work establishes the V28 ratios, scores, exact-three-lane entry condition, or a bend interpretation.

The literature therefore supports testing V28 as a bounded structural prior, but not accepting it from U04 alone and not presenting it as a provenance solution.

V27 promotion boundary

V27 is legitimate but not promoted by the present evidence. A defensible V27-specific general claim requires a future preregistered cohort satisfying all of the following:

at least 30 scans and 15 families;

at least 30 independent V27-triggered components across at least 8 families;

zero components that combine two distinct observed physical axes;

zero TP-to-FN changes and zero marker-admissibility regressions attributable to V27;

recall and exact-count accuracy nondecreasing;

precision and F1 strictly higher than the identical run with V27 disabled;

all program-wide latency, memory, replay, job-success, and family-minimum gates satisfied;

native-resolution adjudication of every triggered component before gold is opened.

With zero false merges among 30 triggered components, the one-sided 95% binomial upper bound on the false-merge probability is approximately 9.5%, corresponding to the program’s minimum 90% line-recall level. Fewer than 30 triggers means insufficient V27-specific promotion evidence, not permission to lower the gate.

The V27 promotion fixture pack must also contain:

two distinct near-parallel, end-to-end axes inside the numerical 6 mm / 20 mm neighborhood;

a transitive chain where A–B and B–C pass pairwise but A–C does not;

endpoint reversal, line-order permutation, and rigid-transform tests.

No such V27 promotion study is authorized in the present tranche; V27 is merely the frozen upstream state required to test V28.

Insertion and effect

The only permitted execution order is:

→ frozen selection
→ V27 selected-fragment stitching
→ V28 bounded-complex paired-return completion

V27 may replace a qualifying connected component of selected segments with one fitted axis. It cannot introduce an unselected candidate.

V28 may execute only when the route is the existing bounded-complex route and the post-V27 selected set contains exactly three physical lanes. It may add at most one axis and may not delete, move, rescore, or relabel any pre-existing selected lane.

V27 predicate

A selected-line graph edge exists exactly when:

axial gap is at most 20 mm.

No clique requirement, support predicate, new residual test, component-size exception, or chain-breaking rule may be introduced.

V28 predicate

All current conditions remain unchanged:

6. REAL-SCAN GATE

Exactly one real scan, R1, is authorized.

Prospective input selection

Before model execution, an independent evaluator must select and seal one scan satisfying all of the following:

acquired after the complete precommit manifest is hashed;

from a part family absent from the 35 LOFO cases, U01–U04, and the 26-pool specificity audit;

produced by the standard acquisition and mesh-generation workflow, with no artificial point deletion or research-only mesh repair;

raw source inspection by the evaluator—not by the model team—shows that one of the four curvature bands is fragmented but genuinely observed;

no hole or opening interrupts the intended counterpart band.

The evaluator may use the drawing and raw scan only to construct this enriched mechanism challenge. The evaluator may not consult V16, V27, V28, candidate-pool, or prediction output when choosing the scan.

The source file is then:

stripped of identity-bearing metadata;

renamed to a random opaque identifier;

SHA-256 hashed;

transferred without drawing, expected count, family, or part identity.

The evaluator separately hashes a sealed envelope containing the drawing, the four reference lines, the existing strict-matcher configuration, and the family-exclusion certificate.

No substitute scan or second acquisition is permitted.

Execution

The opaque scan is processed once through the complete frozen research lane. A second clean-process execution is launched automatically for replay before either output is viewed. The first execution is the official result; the second is solely the precommitted replay check.

Source-only visual adjudication

Before the evaluator envelope is opened, two reviewers inspect the native-resolution source, candidate fragments, post-V27 result, V28 proposal, and cross-sections.

They must unanimously certify only that:

every V27 merge represents one observed surface-axis locus;

V27 leaves exactly three physical lanes;

V28 emits exactly one additional axis;

its source fragments occupy one coherent adjacent return-curvature band;

the proposal is not visibly attached to a hole, perimeter, thickness edge, or unrelated broad-face feature;

the final four-line geometry is source-visually consistent with two return zones on each side.

Their signed judgments and visual-artifact hashes are frozen before unsealing.

Evaluator-reference pass conditions

the V28-added line is a TP under the exact prehashed strict line matcher;

it corresponds to the predeclared missing fourth return axis;

V28 adds no other line;

This is intentionally a mechanism-enriched validation, not an unbiased F1 tranche. Its result must not be appended to the 35-case LOFO table as though it were an independent performance estimate.

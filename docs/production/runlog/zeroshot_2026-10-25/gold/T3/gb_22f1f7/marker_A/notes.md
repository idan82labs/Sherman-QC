# gb_22f1f7 marker A
Full scan of a large L trough (~840 x 245 x 190 mm), t~2.8. 7 bends, all ~90 deg.
- B1 1|3: base to long wall, 839 mm.
- B2 1|5, B3 1|6: base to each end plate (245 mm). Formed radius (apex 2.4 = nominal).
- B4 8|9, B5 7|10: each end plate to its outward top flange (9/12, 10/11), ~240 mm.
- B6 5|15, B7 6|16: each end plate to a short overlap tab (13/15, 14/16) folded 90 deg to lie against the inside of the long wall. Formed radius in the section. I used the outer-skin pair with extent_xyz taken from the inner-skin fold ends (67 mm and 57 mm). B7 is flagged uncertain only because of the <100-point side support.
Rejected: end plate to long wall (3|5, 3|6). The section shows two separate sheet edges meeting at the corner (apex offset 4.6 against the nominal 2.3, no continuous arc), with the overlap tab inside: a joined seam, not a bend. The long slot near the tab fold, the notches in the wall top edge, and the many thin strips (edges) are not bends.
Missed-bend sweep: no flange on the free edges of base or wall.

## Claim boundary

The P-series visuals are encouraging but do not establish F1: trusted manual
line annotations were unavailable, delivery counts were visible, and only P07
was independent of the V51 change. V46–V51 should therefore be treated as a
frozen research candidate, not as evidence that the 90/90/90 semantic gates
have been met.

The central decision is whether a single blind Gold37 run of the already-frozen
candidate is now the most informative action. A poor result is useful and must
be preserved; it would retire V51 transfer without rescue tuning. If a validity
defect requires V52 first, specify exactly one source-only correction that is
motivated by the included code and prospective artifacts without consulting
locked per-case answers.

## Required response format

1. `RULING`: exactly one of the three tokens above. If choosing V52, append a
   short unique architecture name.
2. `VALIDITY ASSESSMENT`: whether a one-shot V51 Gold37 score would be
   interpretable despite delivery-count-contaminated prospective development.
3. `RESEARCH BASIS`: primary research citations and the precise idea adapted.
4. `EXACT ACTION`: for the frozen run, the binding/freeze/scoring order; for
   V52, exact feature names, predicate, insertion point, deterministic ranking,
   and NMS/ownership behavior.
5. `FALSIFICATION GATES`: exact pass/retire conditions. They must include no
   target leakage, byte-identical replay, unchanged point/cache/resource
   contracts, all-case native-resolution visual audit, and the stated final
   semantic thresholds or a clearly justified prospective funding threshold.
6. `EXPECTED FAILURE MODE`: the single most likely systematic error and the
   visual view/overlay that exposes it.
7. `NEXT STEP AFTER FAILURE`: one sentence only; no rescue tuning under the
   same architecture identity.

## Count, localization and zero-shot are different axes

- Exact count asks whether the observed count is correct, independently of where
  predictions are placed. Five predicted lines and five reference bends can
  have the right count while only three lines match: P=R=F1=.60.
- Localized F1 scores one-to-one finite-line matches, penalizing extras and
  misses. Exact localized set is stronger still: all instances correct, no extras.
- A structured count estimate cannot supply line ownership or become observed
  support. CAD's whole-part expected count cannot supply a partial scan's answer.
- For historical two-modality comparisons, use **blind PLY-only** and
  **blind CAD+PLY** as the modality labels. The active offline lane is CAD+scan. CAD
  assistance may still be label-free at inference, but it has additional input.
  Neither mode alone proves unseen-family generalization; previously fitted
  models/thresholds and consumed evaluations remain development history.

The [CAD-guided result](2026-08-22_f1_cad_guided_zero_shot_e2e_result.md) explicitly
distinguishes scan-only zero-shot from CAD-guided zero-shot and labels its

## Active CAD-assisted measurement gaps — source review 2026-09-08

The current [detector/matcher source](../../backend/feature_detection/bend_detector.py)
has issues that count/F1 improvements alone cannot close:

- `_analyze_bend` uses `180 - acos(abs(n1·n2))` in degrees.
  Normal-orientation information is lost; this is not automatically the specified
  forming angle or cylindrical arc sweep.
- `_estimate_bend_radius` can fall back to an angle heuristic and clips fitted
  radii to 0.5–25 mm. `_taubin_circle_fit` returns 3 mm for insufficient/singular
  data. A residual/fit score is not calibrated measurement uncertainty.
- `_find_bend_extent` pads the sampled axial span by 5% at each end. Those
  display/proposal endpoints are not an independently measured physical trim.
- `CADBendExtractor.extract_from_mesh` turns detector estimates/default radii
  into CAD targets and supplies default angle/radius tolerances. These are not
  automatically native nominal dimensions or approved drawing tolerances.
- `_determine_status` can return PASS for a folded bend without radius agreement.
  Legacy status is not the new characteristic-level conformance contract.

These are source findings, not newly executed counterexamples or fixes. The
next connected slice must expose measurement origin/convention and unsupported
characteristics, then compare scan-derived values with independently defined CAD
nominals. Keep CAD expectations, formation state, counts and characteristic
conformance separate. A confidence number, imported CAD or added assessment
schema cannot by itself make an unsupported measurement valid.

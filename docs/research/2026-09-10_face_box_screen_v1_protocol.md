## Reason and conditional mathematical contract

Completed V9 case evidence attributes roughly93.4% of certificate pairs to
initialization, not refinement. Thousands of mostly rejected assignments undergo
full finite-triangle distance certification. This is workload evidence, not a
CPU profile or proof of speedup. The additive helper may reject clearly impossible
support cheaply, but may never accept support or weaken the original distance gate.

For a caller-authenticated face union T contained in box B=[l,u], define
L(p)=max(0,max_j(l_j-p_j,p_j-u_j)). Then L(p)<=distance(p,T). Outward-down
subtraction makes the represented lower bound conservative under the declared
binary64 round-to-nearest/gradual-underflow model. A row with L>distance_gate
is definitely outside. For the COMPLETE support denominator N and outside count O,
an outward-up (N-O)/N bounds any possible inlier fraction. Exclude only if this
upper bound is strictly below the unchanged minimum coverage. Equality and
inconclusive boxes require the original full finite-surface query.

No box certifies face interiors, normals, holes, observed counts, measured answers
or a pose. The same represented transformed points must be used downstream.
The primitive cannot authenticate full triangle enclosure or complete row counts;
its schema explicitly declares those caller obligations. Integration must keep
all original triangle admission/digests, charge every screen row under the existing
work cap and distinguish BOUND_EXCLUDED from SURFACE_QUERIED. It must not report
skipped finite queries as completed or drop hypotheses/denominators.

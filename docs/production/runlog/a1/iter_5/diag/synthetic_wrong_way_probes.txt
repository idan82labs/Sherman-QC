Reading: without fix C a wrong-way reading inside the search window is taken as partly formed the CAD way; its
angle FAILs only because the magnitude is far from nominal, and its lengths and position are reported against a
sharp on the wrong skin. A bend of nominal sweep <= ~11 deg formed fully the wrong way would read its nominal angle
(PASS). With fix C the sign is kept: the angle reads above 180 and always FAILs.

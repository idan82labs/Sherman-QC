# gb_bd09ed, marker B

Large plate (1/2), about 449 x 195 mm, with two big windows and many holes. A flange (3/4), about 22 mm tall, runs along one long edge. The scan is full.

At two places the plate is notched, and the flange material continues past the plate level into a hanging tab with a hole (6/9 and 7/8). Each tab is offset from the flange plane by about one thickness through a joggle (slopes 39/40 and 37/38, about 34 deg each). I checked this on a zoomed labelled section through tab 6/9.

Bends (5):
- B1, 2 to 4: plate to flange, about 90 deg, about 401 mm (about 93 % of the visible fold), fold support 1.0.
  - The default envelope apex landed outside the sharp corner (offset -0.6, apex dot in free air), so I used `apex_mode` `station_median`.
  - With that, the apex offset is 3.0 and the dot sits on the outer arc.
- B2, 3 to 39, and B3, 40 to 9: the joggle above tab 6/9, about 34 and 33 deg, each about 21 mm.
- B4, 3 to 37, and B5, 38 to 7: the joggle above tab 7/8, about 34 and 33 deg.
- Joggles count as 2 lines each.

Rejected:
- 5 and 13: edge faces of the flange top or rail above the flange windows.
- 14 and 19: plate end edge faces.
- 32/56 and 34/52: in-plane plate ears.
- The window, hole and slot walls.
- The small strips 41 to 65.

Doubts:
- The tabs could be read as separate offset plates, but the section shows a continuous formed S, so I counted them as joggles.
- I did not mark a plate-to-tab bend: at the tabs the plate is notched away, and the tabs hang from the flange.

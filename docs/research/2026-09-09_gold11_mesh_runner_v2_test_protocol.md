## Isolation and first gate

One serial named pure suite, under the parent-reviewed supervisor:

- 60 seconds outer wall, 45 seconds worker CPU, 1 GiB process-tree RSS and
  1 MiB retained output; any stricter enclosing bound still applies.
- Isolated Python, no application `conftest`, third-party pytest plugin autoload,
  native opt-in, physical runner CLI, source freeze or unrestricted pytest.
- Whole-runner import is restricted to its inert stdlib top-level declarations.
  No `backend`, OCP, NumPy or SciPy module is imported by this suite. Mock worker
  imports reject any unlisted backend/native import.
- The runner fixture replaces `subprocess.Popen` and `subprocess.run` with
  refusal stubs. Process-table tests replace `run` with invented text only;
  no subprocess or real process-table sampling is performed.
- Writes are confined to bounded invented controls, source sentinels and small
  result files under pytest's supervisor-owned temporary root. Synthetic strings
  called scan/CAD bytes are not valid PLY/STEP inputs and are returned by mocks;
  no mesh/CAD parser or inference executes. The suite never invokes `freeze`,
  `execute`, real `supervise_process`, real reference selection or real scoring.
- No original input, Gold, manifest, retained physical output, frozen attempt,
  reserved incoming-email material or held-out is opened, statted or hashed.
  Test-source and named runner-source reads are the only repository reads here.

Require every runnable case to pass and the sole placeholder to remain skipped,
with no resource stop or source drift. Preserve the first terminal receipt,
output and failures. No automatic rerun, cap change, case removal or promotion
follows a failure. Even a green pure result only qualifies these orchestration
contracts; actual guarded runtime, connected accuracy, exact physical bundle
audit, physical-development output and production/floor gates remain separate.

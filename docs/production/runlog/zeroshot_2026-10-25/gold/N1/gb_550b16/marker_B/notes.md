# gb_550b16 — marker B

**Result: 0 bend lines (no_bends_present).**

Part: a rolled half-cylinder shell (arch), about 210 mm long, radius about 75-80 mm. A separate flat D-shaped bulkhead plate with a long slot sits at mid-length.

## Why no bends
- **Shell.** I took own-tool transverse sections at t = 110 and 250 along the shell axis (axis = bulkhead normal) and measured the normal angle every 4 mm of arc. It changes smoothly, at about 0.6-1.2 deg per 4 mm sample, with no step above about 5 deg anywhere, from one free edge to the other. That is a continuous roll with no localized fold and no flat plates. Segmentation cut it into about 20 mm wide planar strips (3, 5, 7, 10, 4, 6, 8, 12, ...), but these are facets of the curve. Pairing neighbouring strips would give spurious ~10-15 deg "bends".
- **Bulkhead (1/2).** A longitudinal section through the crown shows a separate plate about 2 mm thick, T-joined to the shell with no flange or radius. It is a welded or fitted plate, so its junction with the shell counts as a weld-plate edge, not a bend.
- **Rejected features.**
  - The rows of holes and slots along the crown are hole walls.
  - Two round features on the crown (near patches 51/52) look like embosses or weld nuts.
  - The notch on the bottom edge at the bulkhead and the notch at one end are edges.
  - The slot in the bulkhead is a hole wall.

# gb_10743c, marker B

Motor-mount bracket, a strip about 18 mm wide, t about 2.8 mm. The scan is full. Parts:
- the arm (1/2), with countersunk holes;
- a slope (5/6);
- a short low strip (7/8), parallel to the arm;
- the motor plate (3/4), with a large bore and four holes.

Bends (3):
- B1, 2 to 5: arm to slope, about 40.6 deg, convex on top. About 15.4 mm long, the full fold width.
- B2, 6 to 7: slope to low strip, about 40.6 deg, convex underneath. About 19.2 mm long.
- B3, 7 to 3: low strip to motor plate, about 80 deg, convex outer. About 24.6 mm long.

All three are certain, with fold support 1.0.

Rejected:
- The countersink and emboss rings on the arm, and the hole walls.
- The plate edges 12 and 13, and the thin strips 9 to 11 and 14 to 16, which are edges and arcs.

Doubts: B1 and B2 together look like an offset (two 40 deg bends with opposite convex sides). I counted them as 2 lines, which is the same count the joggle rule would give.
